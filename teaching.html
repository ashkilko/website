The instructor was the most knowledgeable professor I have had at this university and the most engaging.

His approach of always pushing us to get to the ‘why’ of everything enabled me to fully grasp concepts and have a holistic understanding of everything we covered.

He actually taught me different ways to think about things and go about learning subjects.
